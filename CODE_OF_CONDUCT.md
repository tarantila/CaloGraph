# CaloGraph Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CaloGraph is built around personal nutrition and health data. We want its
community to be a safe, respectful, and practical place for people with
different bodies, goals, backgrounds, identities, and levels of technical
experience.

By participating in the CaloGraph repository or its community spaces, you agree
to follow this code of conduct.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, behavior, and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar concepts without condescension.
- Use neutral, nonjudgmental language about food, weight, calorie intake, and
  health goals.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic or thoroughly redacted examples and never ask
  someone to publish health data, credentials, tokens, logs, or backups.
- Distinguish personal experience from medical or nutritional advice. Do not
  present CaloGraph as a diagnostic or treatment tool.
- Consider accessibility and the effects a change may have on different users.
- Respect CaloGraph's license and the licenses and identities of third-party
  projects.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following behavior is not welcome:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, sexualized attention, threats, or
  repeated unwanted contact.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate humiliation, or dismissing
  someone because of their identity, body, health, experience, or ability.
- Publishing another person's private information or identifiable health data
  without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, access tokens, private vulnerability details, or real
  production data in public issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Deliberately disrupting collaboration through spam, trolling, bad-faith
  arguments, or repeated disregard of maintainer guidance.
- Misrepresenting an affiliation with CaloGraph, YAZIO, Apple, or another
  project or company.

This list is illustrative rather than exhaustive. Conduct that undermines a
safe and productive community may still require moderation.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces managed by the CaloGraph project. It also applies when someone publicly
represents the project or acts in an official project role.

## Reporting a problem

Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Contact the repository
maintainer privately through a contact method published on their GitHub
profile. If no private route is available, open an issue containing only a
request for private contact and no details about the incident.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported separately according to
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as
practical. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in
a review is not acceptable. A maintainer involved in a report should not make
the enforcement decision alone.

## Enforcement

Depending on context, severity, and prior behavior, maintainers may:

1. Request a clarification or correction.
2. Edit, hide, or remove content from project-controlled spaces.
3. Issue a private or public warning.
4. Temporarily restrict participation.
5. Permanently ban participation in the project community.

Severe behavior may lead directly to a restriction or ban. Maintainers will
aim for proportionate, consistent decisions while prioritizing the safety and
privacy of affected people. Details of a report or enforcement action may be
withheld to protect those involved.

This is a project-specific code of conduct written for the CaloGraph community.
